Fremont PD switched to the FBI’s incident-based reporting system (NIBRS) in 2022, which can raise counts and change clearance rates compared with earlier years. Comparisons across that line should be made with care.

Totals for the same year can differ between sources: websites that republish FBI-derived data and the police department’s own annual reports count and classify offenses differently. This page uses the state Department of Justice file. Rates per 1,000 residents divide 2024 crimes by the Department of Finance population for Jan 1, 2025 (233,428).
