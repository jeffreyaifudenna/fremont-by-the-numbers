Why this site exists

Fair, free access to public data

Public data should be easy for everyone to find, not just people with money or technical skill. Anyone with time and the right tools can gather these figures. This site does it once, in the open, so no one has to. A more equitable world starts with fair and free access.

It is free, with no account and no paywall. The code and data are open on GitHub. The site runs no surveys of its own: it collects what the City of Fremont, the U.S. Census Bureau, California agencies, Zillow, Redfin and others have already published, and links every number back to them.

Built by JeffreyAiFudenna, with Claude (Anthropic’s AI model) gathering the figures and writing the code. AI can misread a source, so click any number to open its source and check.
